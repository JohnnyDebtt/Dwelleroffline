DWELLER: iPhone OFFLINE EDITION, v16.5 (a one-off copy; it will not get updates)

iPhone can only install a web game from a web address (https), so this folder has to be put online once.
After it is installed on your Home Screen it plays with no connection at all, full screen, and keeps your saves.

1. Put this folder online (free, about 5 minutes, from a computer is easiest):
   - GitHub Pages: make a new public repository, upload every file in this folder (index.html, sw.js,
     manifest.webmanifest and the three icons), then Settings > Pages > "Deploy from a branch" > main / root.
     Your address will be https://YOURNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY/
   - or Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop): drag this whole folder onto the page and sign in to keep the site.
2. On the iPhone, open that address in Safari, wait for the title screen, and play a few seconds (that stores
   the whole game on the phone).
3. Tap Share > "Add to Home Screen" > Add.
4. Open DWELLER from the new icon. Turn the phone sideways. It now works in airplane mode.

Saves live inside the installed app (separate from Safari). Deleting the icon deletes the saves.

This is v16.5: everything up to now, including the new opening (and V on the title to watch it again), the ending
after the Core, their piano music and sounds, the RECORDS page in the pause menu, the first-SEEKER scene that teaches E,
the thread scenes in The Deep, the sliver skits and their replay in the flooded well, and the smoke menus.
On the title screen, TAP HERE - THE OPENING plays the opening again.
If you put it at the same address as the older offline copy, the installed app picks up this version after it has
been opened online once (close it and open it again), and your saves stay. At a new address it installs as a separate app with its own saves
(use Settings > COPY SAVE CODE in the old one and LOAD SAVE CODE in the new one to carry your save over).
